Eagle Eye: Spot the Difference

Privacy Policy

Effective date: 16 October 2026 · Developer: Make It Wrong Studio · Contact: makeitwrong.studio@gmail.com

This policy explains what information is handled when you play Eagle Eye: Spot the Difference ("the game") on Android. The game does not ask you to create an account and does not ask for your name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos, location or any other personal details.

Information stored on your device

Your game progress (levels unlocked, stars earned) and the sound setting are saved only on your phone, inside the app. This information is not sent to us. Uninstalling the game or clearing its data deletes it.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The game shows ads through Google AdMob: a banner at the top of the screen and optional rewarded videos you can choose to watch to get an extra hint. To show and measure ads, Google may collect and process information such as:

  • Your device's advertising ID and other device identifiers
  • General device information (model, operating system, language, screen size)
  • Approximate location derived from your IP address
  • Ad interactions (which ads were shown or tapped) and diagnostic data

This information is collected by Google, not by us, and is handled under Google's Privacy Policy. You can learn how Google uses information from apps that use its services at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

Your choices

  • Personalized ads: on Android, go to Settings → Google → Ads (or Settings → Privacy → Ads) to delete or reset your advertising ID or opt out of personalized ads.
  • European Economic Area, UK and Switzerland: the game asks for your consent before personalized ads are shown. You can change your choice at any time with the Privacy settings link on the game's home screen.
  • Rewarded videos are always optional. Closing one early simply means no extra hint.

Children

The game is designed for a general audience and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information, contact us and we will help remove it.

Security

Data sent by the Google Mobile Ads SDK is transmitted using encryption (HTTPS).

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
